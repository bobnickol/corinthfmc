Who We Are

The Corinth Free Methodist Church has been part of the community since 1870.
We are part of the Free Methodist Church USA and the annual conference of Acts 12:24 churches.
We are active in our community and other ministries in the area.

We are a warm and friendly group of people. The Bible and Prayer are the foundation of this church.
The service is traditional, but in a laid-back way. We sing traditional hymns and spiritual songs together to praise God,
learn from His Word, and encourage each other to live holy lives. We have a children's moment, after the children's moment they go downstairs for children's church.
After worship we meet together for fellowship, you are welcome to join us.

The Gathering Place

The Gathering Place is our church led by Rev. Becky Brady. The Gathering Place meets on Saturdays at 6PM at the Corinth Grange, 531 Main St.
